Our Story

Some stories begin with a grand gesture, while others begin with a simple hello.

Ours began at a gathering of school friends, where we met and were introduced to each other for the very first time. Little did we know that life had a few more beautiful encounters planned for us.

Five months later, we crossed paths again at a friend's wedding. Though life had us living in different cities, fate kept finding little ways to bring us into the same room. Soon, we became part of the same circle of friends, bonding over games, laughter, and spontaneous get-togethers.

And then, after another five months, what began as chance encounters slowly turned into something more meaningful. Despite the miles between us, we decided to begin our journey together, taking a chance on something that felt special.

Looking back, perhaps we were never really strangers — just two people whose paths kept crossing until the timing felt right.

And now, from those unexpected beginnings to the forever ahead, we're writing our favourite chapter yet.
